## Heavy-Duty Hydraulic Water Suppression Networks

Water-based suppression remains the ultimate backbone of structural fire protection. A properly engineered hydrant and sprinkler system ensures immediate localized suppression the moment fire heat trips a bulb or an operator charges a landing valve.

InteGrowSys specializes in the engineering, hydraulic calculations, piping fabrication, pressure testing, and rehabilitation of fire hydrant and automatic sprinkler networks across high-rise commercial complexes, industrial plants, and warehousing facilities.

---

## Technical Scope & Capabilities

### 1. Automatic Sprinkler Systems (IS 15105 / NFPA 13)
- Hydraulic calculations using Hazen-Williams formula to determine pipe diameters, flow velocity, and required pump discharge pressure.
- Installation of standard response, quick response (QR), early suppression fast response (ESFR), and pre-action sprinkler heads (57°C to 141°C ratings).
- Installation of Alarm Check Valves (ACV), water motor gongs, retarding chambers, and inspector test & drain assemblies.
- Flow switch and tamper switch integration with the main fire alarm panel for precise zone identification.

### 2. External & Internal Fire Hydrant Networks (IS 15301 / IS 13039)
- Underground and aboveground mild steel (MS Class C / IS 1239 / IS 3589) ring mains with anti-corrosive wrapping coating.
- Single and double-headed gunmetal landing valves (IS 5290), instantaneous couplings, non-percolating delivery hose reels, and multi-purpose fog nozzles.
- Pillar hydrants, yard hydrants, and external hose cabinets strategically located for quick fire brigade access.
- 4-way and 2-way Fire Brigade Inlet (FBI) breeching connections installed on perimeter roadways.

### 3. Wet Riser & Downcomer Upgrades
- Vertical riser piping with air release valves at the crest, non-return valves, and pressure reducing orifices on lower floors to avoid hose recoil injuries.
- Pressure regulation: Ensuring minimum residual pressure of **3.5 bar** at the highest and furthest hydrant while limiting maximum static pressure to **7 bar** per NBC 2016 Part 4.

## Testing & Hydraulic Diagnostics
- **Hydrostatic Pressure Testing**: Pipeline pressure held at 1.5 times working pressure (minimum 14 to 18 bar) for 2 to 4 hours to verify zero weld or joint leaks.
- **Pitot Tube Discharge Flow Tests**: Verifying minimum discharge of **900 LPM** per hydrant branch and correct automatic cut-in of the fire pumps.
- **Flushing & Descaling**: Remediation of internal sediment, rust buildup, and choked pipe bends in aging facilities.
