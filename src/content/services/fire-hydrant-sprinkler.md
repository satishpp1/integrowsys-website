## Water-based fire protection

Hydrant and sprinkler systems must be designed and maintained to match the building, hazard classification, water supply and applicable requirements.

- Review of existing hydrant and sprinkler infrastructure
- Scope development for new work or extensions
- Pipework, valves, accessories and equipment coordination
- Inspection, maintenance and documentation support
- Coordination with pump room and water storage requirements

Pipe sizing, pump duty, hydraulic calculations and layout must be determined from verified site data and the applicable design basis.
