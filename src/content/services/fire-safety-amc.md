## Planned maintenance

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) can provide a defined schedule of visits and a consistent record of equipment condition and follow-up actions.

A scope may include, subject to the agreed contract:

- Fire alarm system checks and observations
- Extinguisher inspection records
- Hydrant, sprinkler and pump-system checks
- Fault and deficiency reporting
- Corrective-action recommendations
- Visit reports and maintenance history

Equipment quantities, visit frequency, consumables, spare parts, refill charges, emergency call-outs and exclusions should be itemized in the commercial proposal.
