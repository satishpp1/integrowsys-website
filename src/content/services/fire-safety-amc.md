## Comprehensive, SLA-Backed Fire Safety AMC

Fire safety equipment is rarely used, but when an emergency occurs, every valve, pressure switch, and detector must respond within seconds. An InteGrowSys Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) transforms passive compliance into active operational reliability.

We deliver structured monthly and quarterly preventive maintenance covering every layer of your life safety infrastructure—hydrants, sprinklers, diesel fire pumps, addressable panels, clean agent gas cylinders, and emergency exits—guaranteeing statutory adherence under the **Maharashtra Fire Prevention & Life Safety Measures Act 2006**.

---

## Planned Maintenance Schedule & Scope

### Monthly Preventive Maintenance
- Functional trip test of fire alarm manual call points (MCPs) on rotation across zones.
- Main electrical pump, jockey pump, and diesel engine test runs under simulated pressure drops.
- Inspection of pump room suction and delivery valve status (open/closed padlock checks).
- Verification of panel battery voltage, charging current, and mains power failover.
- Visual check of pressure gauges, flow switches, and line pressure stabilization.

### Quarterly In-Depth Servicing
- Deep cleaning and aerosol smoke testing of smoke/heat detectors.
- Diesel engine lubrication check, fuel line inspection, filter cleaning, and cranking battery specific gravity testing.
- Full flow test from topmost and furthest yard hydrants, measuring residual pressure with pitot gauge.
- Inspection of sprinkler alarm valves, test & drain valves, and water motor gongs.
- Extinguisher weight checks, pressure gauge audits, and discharge nozzle clearance.

### Bi-Annual / Annual Compliance Audits
- Complete hydraulic pressure test of fire hydrant wet riser pipelines.
- Comprehensive gap analysis against the site’s approved Fire NOC drawings.
- Verification and signing of statutory **Form-B** certificates required by local municipal fire authorities (PMC, PCMC, MIDC, PMRDA).
