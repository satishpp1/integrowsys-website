## Coordinated building systems

Fire and life safety often depend on more than one piece of equipment. Integration planning can help clarify the intended sequence, signals and handover between systems.

Potential areas include:

- Staircase pressurization systems
- Lift passage or lobby pressurization
- Basement ventilation and smoke-control interfaces
- Fire alarm interface signals
- Selected equipment run/fault status

The final sequence, interlocks, cause-and-effect matrix, control method and acceptance testing must be verified for the specific building and design.
