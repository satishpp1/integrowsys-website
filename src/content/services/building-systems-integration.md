## Coordinated Life Safety & Building Automation

During a fire, toxic smoke and heat represent the primary danger to human life—not flames alone. An effective building defense requires fire alarms, ventilation fans, motorized dampers, and elevators to execute an orchestrated sequence within seconds of detector activation.

InteGrowSys designs, wires, tests, and certifies mission-critical Building Systems Integration and Smoke Management interfaces, bridging the gap between standalone fire panels and central Building Management Systems (BMS).

---

## Key Integration Subsystems

### 1. Staircase & Lift Lobby Pressurization (NBC 2016 Part 4)
- **Positive Pressure Regulation**: Interlocking fire alarm trip relays with heavy-duty axial centrifugal pressurization blowers.
- Maintains positive pressure (between **25 Pa to 50 Pa**) inside emergency stairwells and lift lobbies relative to the fire floor, creating an impenetrable aerodynamic barrier against smoke infiltration.
- Barometric relief dampers and differential pressure sensor calibration to ensure exit doors can still be opened easily by fleeing occupants (maximum door opening force < 133 N).

### 2. Primary & Secondary Elevator Homing (Lift Grounding)
- Potential-free relay interlocks connected directly to elevator machine room (EMR) group controllers.
- Automatic recall: On fire alarm activation, passenger and service elevators immediately cancel existing floor calls, travel non-stop to the pre-designated ground exit floor, park with doors held open, and lock out hall buttons.
- Secondary floor homing if the primary lobby detector is in alarm.
- Dedicated Fireman’s Lift control switch operation verification.

### 3. HVAC Fire Dampers & Basement Smoke Extraction
- Interlocking fire alarm zones with motorized fire and smoke dampers (MFSD) using 24V DC / 230V AC actuators with thermal fusible links.
- Instant shutdown of comfort Air Handling Units (AHU) on the affected fire floor to prevent toxic smoke recirculation through central ducting.
- Activation of basement jet fans and heavy-duty dual-speed smoke extract fans (rated for **250°C / 2 hours**) to clear basement carparks for emergency responders.

### 4. Access Control Emergency Fail-Safe Release
- Fire alarm dry-contact interfacing with access control power supply units (PSU).
- Cuts power immediately to magnetic electromagnetic locks (EM locks) and motorized turnstiles across all exit paths, allowing unimpeded emergency egress.

## Cause-and-Effect Matrix Verification
Every integration project includes a formal Cause-and-Effect (C&E) Matrix test protocol. InteGrowSys engineers physically trip each individual detector loop, verifying that only the correct combination of fans, dampers, lifts, and doors respond as approved by the local fire officer.
