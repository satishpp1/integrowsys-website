## What this service covers

Fire alarm systems help detect and signal potential fire events. The right scope depends on occupancy, system type, installed equipment, site requirements and applicable codes.

- Review of existing conventional or addressable fire alarm equipment
- Installation, extensions or modifications where agreed
- Fault investigation and troubleshooting
- Interface coordination with selected building systems
- Maintenance and service reporting

## Existing systems

Before recommending a replacement, an assessment can review panel condition, device status, available interfaces, documentation and the practicality of repair or upgrade. Any change to a life-safety system must preserve approved operation and be performed by suitably qualified personnel.

## Request a site assessment

Share the panel make/model, site location, current issue and approximate device count so the scope can be reviewed.
