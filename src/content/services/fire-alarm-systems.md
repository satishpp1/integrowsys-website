## Comprehensive Fire Detection & Alarm Solutions

Fire alarm systems serve as the critical first line of defense for commercial, industrial, and high-occupancy facilities. InteGrowSys provides end-to-end engineering support—from fresh system design and statutory code compliance to troubleshooting stubborn ground faults and retrofitting older legacy panels with smart cloud alerts.

Whether your facility operates multi-loop intelligent addressable networks across multiple buildings or localized conventional multi-zone panels, our engineers ensure your installation aligns with **IS 2189:2018**, **National Building Code (NBC) 2016 Part 4**, and **NFPA 72** standards.

---

## What Our Service Covers

### 1. Addressable & Conventional Panel Engineering
- Complete loop design, device addressing, detector spacing, and decibel-level acoustic planning for sounder/strobes.
- Supply and integration of intelligent multi-criteria smoke/heat sensors, beam detectors for high-bay areas, and manual call points (MCP).
- Interfacing with third-party systems: primary lift grounding (homing), HVAC fire damper trip, access control fail-safe door release, and voice evacuation systems (PA/VA).

### 2. Panel Diagnostics & Fault Troubleshooting
- Resolution of open-circuit faults, short circuits, loop communication bottlenecks, and earth ground leakages.
- Replacement and reprogramming of damaged loop cards, motherboard modules, and power supply units (PSU).
- Standby battery calculation, load testing, and charger circuit calibration ensuring mandatory 24-hour backup + 30-minute alarm sounding.

### 3. Non-Invasive Smart Monitoring Retrofit (AlertGuard™)
- Interface potential-free Form-C contacts (Common Fire, Common Fault, Power Fail) with our AlertGuard IoT gateway.
- Instant alert dispatch to facility managers and safety officers via Email and Webhook the moment a detector trips—without voiding existing panel approvals.

## Statutory Compliance & Documentation

All fire alarm design and audit activities are executed in strict alignment with:
- **NBC 2016 Part 4 (Life Safety)**: Clause 3.4.12 occupant load and zone requirements.
- **IS 2189:2018**: Code of practice for selection, installation, and maintenance of automatic fire detection and alarm systems.
- **Maharashtra Fire Prevention & Life Safety Measures Act**: Statutory inspection records for bi-annual **Form-B** certificate issuance.
