## Fire pump support

Fire pump systems are a critical part of water-based fire protection. Service scope should be based on the installed arrangement, controller, pump ratings, water supply and approved design.

- Pump room and equipment review
- Main, jockey and standby pump arrangement review
- Controller and status-signal assessment
- Planned checks and service documentation
- Potential status monitoring where suitable outputs are available

Any testing or modification must follow manufacturer instructions, site safety controls and applicable requirements.
