## The Heart of Water-Based Fire Suppression

The fire pump room is the mechanical core that powers hydrants, wet risers, and sprinkler systems across an entire campus. In an emergency, fire pumps must start automatically within seconds of a line pressure drop and sustain continuous discharge without cavitation or power interruptions.

InteGrowSys provides full lifecycle engineering for fire pump rooms: from initial capacity calculation and pump alignment to automated controller rewiring, diesel engine rehabilitation, and IoT status monitoring.

---

## Standard Three-Pump System Architecture

We engineer and maintain full pump rooms conforming to **IS 12469**, **IS 15469**, and **NBC 2016 Part 4**:

### 1. Main Electrical Fire Pump (Motor-Driven)
- High-head centrifugal split-case or end-suction pump powered by an industrial squirrel-cage motor.
- Fully wired Automatic Star-Delta or Soft Starter controller with ammeters, voltmeters, and single-phase prevention.
- Automatically cuts in when jockey pump cannot sustain system pressure drop caused by an open hydrant or sprinkler.

### 2. Standby Diesel Engine Fire Pump
- Compression ignition industrial diesel engine ensuring 100% suppression readiness even during complete grid power blackouts.
- Automatic battery charging console with dual heavy-duty 12V/24V lead-acid batteries, solenoid fuel valves, and heat exchangers.
- Automatic crank controller executing up to 3 automatic cranking attempts with emergency manual mechanical override lever.

### 3. Jockey Maintenance Pump
- High-pressure multistage vertical pump designed to absorb minor seasonal pressure fluctuations and minor valve weepage without starting the heavy main pumps.
- Controlled via dual adjustable pressure switches with narrow cut-in and cut-out hysteresis bands.

## Common Pump Room Issues We Solve
- **Frequent Jockey Hunting**: Identifying and isolating concealed underground ring main leaks, leaking check valves, or ruptured diaphragm tanks.
- **Diesel Engine Failures**: Troubleshooting gummed fuel injectors, dead starting batteries, jammed starter relays, or choked raw water cooling loops.
- **Controller Relay Chattering**: Upgrading corroded mechanical contactors and burnt control transformers with modern solid-state timing relays.
- **Suction Cavitation & Vibration**: Re-aligning flexible couplings, replacing damaged mechanical shaft seals, and clearing suction strainer debris.
