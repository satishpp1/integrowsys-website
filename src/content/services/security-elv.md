## Integrated Electronic Security & Facility Infrastructure

Modern facilities require unified control over physical security, asset protection, and personnel movement without creating friction for everyday business operations. InteGrowSys designs, deploys, and maintains enterprise-grade Extra Low Voltage (ELV) and physical security infrastructure for industrial plants, IT campuses, and commercial properties.

We ensure that your CCTV, biometric access, perimeter protection, and structured data cabling integrate harmoniously with your life safety and emergency response procedures.

---

## Core ELV & Security Capabilities

### 1. Enterprise IP CCTV Surveillance
- High-definition IP network cameras: Fixed domes, outdoor bullet cameras with infrared (IR) night vision, and pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) optical zoom domes for perimeter perimeters.
- Network Video Recorder (NVR) and Video Management System (VMS) architecture with scalable storage arrays (30, 60, or 90 days retention per statutory requirements).
- AI video analytics: Perimeter virtual tripwires, loitering detection, vehicle Automated Number Plate Recognition (ANPR), and blocked emergency exit detection.

### 2. Access Control & Time Attendance
- Biometric facial recognition terminals, multi-technology RFID smart card readers (HID, Mifare, Desfire), and fingerprint scanners.
- Flap barriers, optical turnstiles, and motorized swing gates for commercial lobbies and campus entry gates.
- Direct integration with fire alarm outputs for instantaneous fail-safe emergency release, ensuring all turnstiles drop and EM locks de-energize during an alarm.

### 3. Public Address & Voice Alarm (PA/VA)
- Multi-zone public address systems with ceiling speakers, sound projector horns, and wall-mount units for factories and warehouses.
- Automated phased evacuation messaging: Broadcasts clear pre-recorded evacuation instructions to the fire floor and floor above, while advising other floors to stand by.
- Emergency paging console override with priority microphone in the central security control room.

### 4. Structured Networking & Fiber Optic Backbones
- Cat6 / Cat6A structured data and voice cabling with certified cable trays, patch panels, and server rack management.
- Multi-strand single-mode and multi-mode fiber optic cabling connecting distributed pump rooms, gatehouses, and remote sub-stations to the main command center.
