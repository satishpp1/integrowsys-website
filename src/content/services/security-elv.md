## Facility security and connectivity

InteGrowSys can discuss CCTV, access control and networking requirements alongside the wider facility infrastructure.

- CCTV camera and recording requirements
- Access-control points and user workflows
- Structured networking and connectivity coordination
- Integration considerations with other facility systems where appropriate

Camera coverage, retention, network capacity, cybersecurity, privacy and site permissions should be considered during design. Final equipment, licensing and scope are confirmed in the quotation.
