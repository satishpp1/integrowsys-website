## Practical, Engaging Workplace Safety Training

The most sophisticated fire alarm panel or sprinkler network is only as effective as the people on site during an emergency. In critical moments, hesitation and panic cause costly delays. InteGrowSys provides practical, immersive fire safety training tailored to your facility layout, workforce profile, and specific operational hazards.

Our training modules are delivered by certified safety professionals and former fire officers, combining classroom scenario briefings with controlled outdoor hands-on extinguisher practice and full evacuation drills.

---

## Training Modules & Curriculum

### Module 1: Fire Prevention & Chemistry of Fire
- Understanding fire tetrahedron: Fuel, Heat, Oxygen, and Chemical Reaction.
- Classification of fires (Class A, B, C, D, and Electrical fires per IS 15683).
- Common workplace ignition sources: electrical overloading, hot work, pantry hazards, battery hubs, and improper storage.

### Module 2: The P.A.S.S. Technique & Hands-On Extinguisher Drill
- Theory and correct operation of ABC Dry Chemical Powder, CO2 gas, Mechanical Foam (AFFF), and Clean Agent extinguishers.
- Supervised live fire extinguishing drill: Every participant operates an extinguisher against a controlled test fire using the **Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep (P.A.S.S.)** method.
- Understanding limitations: When to fight a fire and when to evacuate immediately.

### Module 3: Floor Warden & Emergency Response Team (ERT) Protocol
- Roles and responsibilities of Floor Marshals, Evacuation Wardens, and Incident Commanders.
- Evacuation protocol: Checking restrooms, conference rooms, server areas, and accounting for all personnel at muster points.
- Assisting differently-abled occupants and handling panic or crowd bottlenecks in emergency stairwells.

### Module 4: Mock Evacuation Drills & Observation Audits
- Complete site evacuation drill coordinated with facility security, BMS operators, and HR teams.
- Verification of alarm intelligibility, stairwell clearance, exit discharge speed, and roll-call assembly time.
- Post-drill debriefing report detailing total evacuation duration, bottlenecks observed, and recommendations for improvement.
