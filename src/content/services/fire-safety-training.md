## Training that engages participants

Training can be adapted to the workplace, workforce size, building layout and emergency plan. Depending on the agreed scope, topics may include:

- Fire prevention and common ignition risks
- How to recognize and respond to alarms
- Fire extinguisher awareness and supervised hands-on practice
- Evacuation routes, assembly points and accountability
- Assistance for people who need support during evacuation
- Scenario-based discussion, knowledge checks and drill observations

Practical demonstrations require a controlled area, appropriate supervision and agreed consumables. The site emergency plan and local procedures remain authoritative.
