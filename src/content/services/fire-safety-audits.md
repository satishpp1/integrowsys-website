## Pragmatic, Engineering-First Fire Risk Audits

Statutory compliance is not about ticking boxes on an outdated checklist—it is about identifying real, physical vulnerabilities that could prevent safe evacuation or disable fire suppression when minutes matter most.

InteGrowSys conducts comprehensive on-site Fire Safety Audits and Risk Assessments for commercial high-rises, industrial plants, IT parks, and logistics centers across Pune and Maharashtra. Our reports provide clear, prioritized engineering roadmaps distinguishing between immediate life-safety hazards and long-term capital upgrade recommendations.

---

## What We Audit on Site

### 1. Means of Egress & Evacuation Routes
- Measurement of travel distances to exits against NBC 2016 Part 4 Table 5 occupant loads.
- Staircase enclosure integrity, fire door rating (120-minute FD120), panic hardware operation, and self-closing hinges.
- Emergency exit illumination lux levels, photo-luminescent signage visibility, and clearance of escape corridors.

### 2. Active Fire Protection Systems
- Physical inspection and static/residual pressure checks of hydrant networks and sprinkler installations.
- Visual inspection and trip test of fire alarm panels, detectors, manual call points, and notification sounders.
- Fire pump room operating readiness, auto-sequencing checks, and emergency fuel storage compliance.
- Portable fire extinguisher adequacy, hydrostatic test dates, and distribution per IS 2190 standards.

### 3. Electrical & Industrial Fire Hazards
- Thermal imaging (thermography) recommendations for main LT panels, transformer yards, and busbar junctions.
- Hazardous chemical storage compliance, paint booth ventilation, diesel generator fuel piping, and battery room ventilation.
- Clean agent fire suppression systems in server rooms, UPS hubs, and electrical control rooms (Novec / FM-200 / Inergen).

## The Audit Deliverables & Report
- **Executive Summary & Risk Scoring**: High, Medium, Low severity risk classification matrix.
- **Visual Photographic Evidence**: High-resolution annotated site photographs documenting every identified deficiency.
- **Code Reference Citations**: Specific references to NBC 2016 Part 4, relevant Indian Standards (IS), and Maharashtra Fire Rules.
- **Budgeted Remediation Roadmap**: Actionable, pragmatic engineering fixes with estimated implementation effort and priority.
- **Statutory Form-B Gap Review**: Comprehensive readiness assessment prior to local fire officer annual inspections.
