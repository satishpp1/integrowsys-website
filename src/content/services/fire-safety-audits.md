## A practical site review

A fire safety audit or gap assessment can help facility teams organize observations and prioritize follow-up. The scope, method and limitations should be agreed before the visit.

- Review of visible fire protection and alarm arrangements
- Access and housekeeping observations
- Extinguisher and emergency-equipment record review
- Evacuation signage and emergency readiness observations
- Prioritized findings and recommendations

A general checklist is not a substitute for a site inspection, engineering assessment or legally required certification.
